# Find Steve Collage

## Product identity

The public index is a giant, ever-growing collage of Steve cutouts. It is a game, a cultural archive, and a piece of internet art. The collage is the product. Finding a particular Steve is one way to interact with it.

This is not a data visualization of Steves in popular culture. Charts and study pages remain available as editorial side rooms. Visitors start in the pile.

## Experience

There are a lot of Steves on TV. Find this one.

A chaotic canvas of cut-out Steves from movies, television, and commercials. New Steves are added continually. Some are famous. Some appeared in one commercial in 1987. All of them belong.

The visual reference is an absurd pop-culture scrapbook, not a traditional Where's Waldo illustration. Different eras, lighting, resolutions, outfits, and film grain sit next to each other. Some Steves are tiny, some enormous, some partly covered by other Steves. Visual inconsistency is the charm.

### 1. Find the Steve

A floating card presents a randomly selected Steve using the exact cutout hidden on the canvas. Click the correct Steve to win, then get another target. A challenge link can lock the target so a friend is asked to find the same Steve.

### 2. Explore the Steves

Hover reveals identity. Click opens a small information card: name, work, when they appeared, and a link to the original scene or source when available. During an active find round, a click first checks whether the Steve is the target. On mobile, a first tap identifies and checks for a match; a second action opens the full card.

### 3. Get lost in the collage

Pan and zoom. The canvas expands as the collection grows. Come back later and there are more Steves crowded into it.

### 4. Add a Steve

People nominate missing Steves through the existing submission path. Editorial review still publishes the archive record. Cutouts are added through the internal studio, not by public upload of stills.

## Visual rules

- Dense, layered, maximalist. Almost no empty space.
- Photographic cutouts, never a consistent illustrated set.
- Clusters, mixed scales, and overlap. Do not append new images in a rightward strip.
- Every Steve needs enough uncovered, clickable silhouette to be findable. Hits use the saved alpha mask, not the bounding box.
- The public collage only shows cutouts whose image rights have been reviewed and marked displayable.

## What not to build yet

No accounts, currencies, achievements, or complicated game menus. Share the collage crop or a find-this-Steve link before sharing a score. The living artifact is the reason to return, not a leaderboard.
